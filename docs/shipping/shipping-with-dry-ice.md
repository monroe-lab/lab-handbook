# Shipping with dry ice

**Before You Begin**
In order to ship **hazardous materials** (**Category B**, **excepted quantities**, **dry ice**), training is required **every two years** and must be completed in the UC Learning Center. Complete the required LMS training courses to gain access to AggieShip. Once certified, log into AggieShip to generate your FedEx shipping label and forms.

**Supplies Needed**

* A styrofoam and a cardboard box (reuse company packaging; deface all existing exterior labels)
* Dry ice — available from the Chemistry Store in the Chemistry Building
* Plastic envelope for shipping forms
* Printed FedEx label (generated via AggieShip)
* Printed [dry ice label](https://www.fedex.com/content/dam/fedex/us-united-states/services/Dry_Ice_Label.pdf) or sticker
* Shipping manifest stating samples are non-hazardous and non-toxic

*Note: If samples are hazardous or toxic, additional contacts and procedures are required — consult your supervisor before proceeding.*
**Packing**

1. Place samples inside the styrofoam inner box with dry ice.
2. Place the packing list between the outer cardboard box and the inner styrofoam — not inside the styrofoam.
3. Place the shipping manifest and FedEx label in the plastic envelope and affix to the outside of the box.
4. Deface all old labels on the exterior of the box.

**Drop-Off**

* **Preferred:** Drop off at Hoagland Hall Reprographics — FedEx picks up there daily (early in the day, probably before 3 pm).
* **Alternative:** PES has FedEx pickup only on days when they also have a drop-off.
