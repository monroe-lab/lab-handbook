# rCutSmart Buffer

NEB's universal 10X restriction digest buffer, supplied with (and used for) [[dpni]] in
[[dpni-methylation-check]]. B6004S is 5 mL of the 10X stock.

> ℹ️ **Composition (1X)**
>
> | Component | Concentration |
> | --- | --- |
> | Potassium acetate | 50 mM |
> | Tris-acetate | 20 mM |
> | Magnesium acetate | 10 mM |
> | Recombinant albumin | 100 µg/mL |
> | pH | 7.9 @ 25 °C |

> 💡 **Lab use**
>
> Supplied as 10X — dilute to 1X in the digest. Recombinant albumin is already included, so do
> not add BSA separately.
