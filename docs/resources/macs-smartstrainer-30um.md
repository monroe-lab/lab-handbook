# MACS SmartStrainer, 30 µm

30 µm cell strainer used as the **final filtration step in nuclei isolation** — it passes
released nuclei while retaining tissue debris, cell-wall fragments, and clumps.

Specified in the published plant Fiber-seq methods and used by
[[fiber-seq-nuclei-isolation]].

> 💡 **Lab use**
>
> The 30 µm strainer is the fine step. Coarser pre-filtration through a
> [[cell-strainer-70um]] or miracloth comes first — going straight to 30 µm with unfiltered
> homogenate clogs it and costs you nuclei.
>
> Filtration quality directly sets Fiber-seq data quality: intact, debris-free nuclei are what
> the Hia5 reaction needs. Check a filtered aliquot on the scope before proceeding.
