# Promega Wizard HMW DNA Extraction Kit

HMW genomic DNA extraction kit. **Never tried in this lab** — recorded here because it is what
both published plant Fiber-seq papers used, which makes it the most credible untested
alternative to the lab's CTAB default.

> 💡 **Why it is on this list**
>
> The lab's own comparison ran CTAB against NEB columns ([[monarch-spin-gdna-extraction-kit]])
> and picked CTAB. The published work did neither — it used this kit. That is a real gap in the
> lab's evidence, not a settled question.
>
> If CTAB yield or purity becomes the bottleneck, this is the first thing to try, and it comes
> with the advantage that a published Fiber-seq result already exists downstream of it.

> ⚠️ **Untested here**
>
> No in-lab data. Do not present this as a validated route. If someone runs it, record the
> FemtoPulse trace and Qubit alongside a CTAB control on [[fiber-seq-hmw-extraction]].
