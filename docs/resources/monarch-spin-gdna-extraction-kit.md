# Monarch Spin gDNA Extraction Kit

Column-based genomic DNA extraction kit. **Tried by the lab for Fiber-seq HMW extraction and
abandoned** in favor of CTAB — this card exists to record that, so the decision does not get
re-litigated.

> ⚠️ **Not the current method for Fiber-seq**
>
> Samples FS_1, FS_6, and FS_10S were extracted on NEB columns; FS_T3_3 was extracted with
> CTAB. Reviewing the traces, Noravit Chumchim at the Genome Center wrote that *"the damage
> here is likely caused by the extraction method"* and *"I would highly suggest using DNA from
> the same extraction method as sample 4"* — sample 4 being the CTAB extraction.
>
> Column chemistry is convenient but shears and appears to damage HMW plant DNA. See
> [[fiber-seq-hmw-extraction]] for the current default.

> 💡 **Still useful for**
>
> Ordinary genotyping-grade gDNA where fragment length does not matter. The problem is
> specific to HMW/long-read applications, not the kit in general.
