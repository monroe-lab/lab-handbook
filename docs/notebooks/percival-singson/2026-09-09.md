## Plan for today

CTAB extraction of grape feeder cells with the G2 RNA/DNA enhancer

Do pre-wash protocol of the cells before starting CTAB

## What I did

Amounts to use are 0.5mL, 1mL (990ul), 1.25mL, 1.5mL, and of sample

1. Defrosted 5/11 DT Monday A. Added 0.1g PVP36 to 10mL premade CTAB lysis buffer stock.
2. Pipetted 2mL of sample into 4 2mL tubes and centrifuged at 14000rpm for 2 min.
3. Poured out supernatant and pipetted leftover liquid out. Resuspened in 1.5mL sucrose wash by pipetting and vortexing (need to really break up pellet.) Centrifuged for 1 min at 14000rpm. (Do 2 min next time, didn't fully pellet all samples)
4. Poured off supernatant, resuspended each in 1.5mL ultrapure DI water.
5. Pipetted 0.5mL, 990uL, 1.25mL, and 1.5mL into new tubes (0.5mL into a 2mL tube and the rest into 15mL tubes.)
6. Added 500uL of the G2 DNA/RNA enhancer into each.
7. Added 10uL 10% SDS into each.
8. Added 12uL RNase A, 3.5uL Proteinase K, and 500uL CTAB lysis buffer into each. Vortexed breifly to mix. Placed in 55C incubation (larger tubes are in the large incubator w/less precise temperature, may have been hotter) for 20 minutes.
9. Centrifuged 2mL tube at 4400 g/rcf for 10 minutes at room temperature. Centrifuged 15mL tubes at 4000 g/rcf for 10 minutes at room temperature.
10. Removed 1.5mL supernatant from 0.5mL tube, 2mL from 1mL tube, 1.99mL from the 1.25mL tube, and 2.458mL from the 1.5mL tube. Transferred all supernatants to new labeled 15mL tubes.
11. Added an equal volume of CI to each tube and inverted 20 times to mix. Incubated at room temperature on the Hula mixer for 10 minutes.
12. Transferred upper aqueous phase from each tube into 1.5mL tubes. (We do not have a magnetic rack for tubes larger than 1.5mL, so each tube had 2 tubes of 750uL of each phase taken. The rest was discarded.) Placed on hula mixer to incubate at room temp for 30 minutes.
