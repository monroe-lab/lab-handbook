## What I did

Reached out to Foundation Plant Services about obtaining 1103 Paulsen tissue (leaf and/or immature flower.) Looking into potentially obtaining from USDA-ARS germplasm as well.

 

Sample sizes to use 0.25mL, 0.5mL, 1mL

1. Defrosted 2/17/26 DT Monday A (the first sample collected).
2. Added 0.1g PVP36 to 10mL premade CTAB stock.
3. Pipetted 2mL of sample into two 2mL tubes and spun down for 2 min at 14000rcf. Removed most but not all of supernatant.
4. Resuspended pellets in 1.5mL sucrose wash by pipetting and vortexing, then centrifuged for 2 min at 14000 rcf. Removed supernatant.
5. Repeated step 3. (maybe in future use less volume, had some overflow problems.)
6. Resuspended pellets in 1.5mL ultrapure DI H2O by pipetting and vortexing.
7. Removed 250uL, 500uL, and 900uL of the cell suspension into 3 labeled 2mL tubes.
8. Added 12.5uL RNase A, 3.5uL Proteinase K, 500uL G2 RNA/DNA enhancer, 10uL 10% SDS, and 500uL CTAB buffer to each tube then pipetted to mix. Placed in 55C incubator for 20 minutes (added 5 min to time because incubator was not yet at temperature.)
9. Centrifuged for 10 min at 4400 rcf. Removed supernatant and placed into new labeled tubes. 0.25- 1mL removed, 0.5- 1200 uL, 0.9mL-1200 uL.
10. Had Eric finish the experiment
