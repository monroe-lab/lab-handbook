## What I did

Amounts to test: 250ul, 500ul, 1mL
Sample: 4/22/26 Wednesday B LJ

1. Pipetted 5mL of cell culture into a 50 mL falcon tube and then centrifuged at 4500rpm for 5 minutes. 
2. Poured out supernatant and added sugar wash to equal 10mL volume. Pipetted to resuspend and then vortexed before centrifuging for 10min at 1000rpm.
3. Removed supernatant and top charcoal particles from pellet. Resuspended pellet in 10mL volume sugar wash, vortexed, and then centrifuged at 1000rpm for 5 min.
4. Repeated step 4.
5. Repeated step 4. 
6. Repeated step 4 but for 3 min instead of 5.
7. Repeated step 6.
8. Repeated step 6.
9. Removed entire pellet and resuspended in 10mL sugar wash. Centrifuged at 1000g for 5 min. 
10. Repeated step 9, except centrifuged for 3 min.
11. Repeated step 10.
12. Contents to the tube were then pipetted in 1mL, 500ul, and 250ul amounts into labeled 2mL tubes.
13. 10 ul of SDS 10%, 500ul CTAB lysis buffer, 12ul RNase A, and 3.5ul Proteinase K were added to each of the 2ml tubes. Pipetted gently to mix, then incubated at 56 C for 20 minutes.
14. Removed from incubator then centrifuged at 4400 g/rcf for 10 minutes.
15. Remove supernatant and place into a new tube.
    1. 1ml- 1ml removed
    2. 500ul- 700ul removed
    3. 250ul-460 ul removed
16. Add an equal volume of room temp CI to the tubes. Invert tubes 20 times, then incubate for 10 min at room temp on the hula mixer.
17. Centrifuge at 5000 g/rcf for 10 min at RT.
18. Remove upper aqueous part into a new 1.5mL tube in the chemical hood, keeping track of how much removed
    1. 1mL-800ul
    2. 500ul-600ul
    3. 250ul- 400ul 
19. Add an equal volume to the removed liquid of 0.4% SeraMag beads to the tubes and mix by inversion 20-25 times then place on the Hula mixer for 30 min at RT. Prewarm the EB in the meantime
20. Spin tubes down at 5000 g/rcf for 1 min. Then place on magnetic rack and wait until the solution becomes clear, then discard the supernatant.
21. Wash 2x with 80% EtOH, discarding after each wash
22. Add 30-50ul (40ul was used) prewarmed EB buffer to the tubes and wait a few min, before moving to a new labeled tube.
23. Tubes were placed in the freezer to be quantified at a later date.
