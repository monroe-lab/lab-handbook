## What I did

1. Defrosted 5/28/26 JD B. Added 0.1g of PVP to 10mL of CTAB stock.
2. Pipetted 1mL and 500uL suspension culture into two 2mL tubes. Added 100uL PBS to each, vortexed breifly to mix, then let sit at room temp for 10 minutes.
3. Added 10uL 10% SDS to each sample, then vortexed. Added 12uL RNase A, 3.5 uL Proteinase K, and 500uL CTAB buffer to each sample, then pipetted to mix. Incubated at 56C for 20 min.
4. Added 12ul RNase A to each, pipetted to mix, then placed back in 56C incubation for 15 min.
5. Centrifuged for 10 min at 4400 g/rcf. 
6. Removed supernatant and placed in a new labeled 2mL tube.
    1. 1mL- 1100uL removed
    2. 500uL- 900uL removed
7. Added an equal volume CI to each tube and then inverted 20 times to mix, then placed on the hula mixer for 10 minutes. 
8. Centrifuged tubes at 5000 g/rcf for 10 min. Removed upper aqueous layer and placed into new 1.5mL tubes.
    1. 1mL- 700uL removed
    2. 500uL- 600uL removed
9. Added an equal volume of 0.4% SeraMag beads to each tube, and then inverted 20-25 times before incubating on the hula mixer at room temp for 30 min.
10. Tubes were then spun down for 1 min at 5000 g/rcf and then placed on a magnetic rack until solution became clear. Supernatant was removed and discarded.
11. 80% EtOH was added to both tubes and the beads were resuspended using a pipette, vortexed, then spun down and placed back on the magnetic rack until the solution became clear. Discarded the supernatant.
12. Repeated step 11. 
13. Added 40uL prewarmed EB buffer to each tube, resuspended with the pipette, vortexed, then spun down and placed on the magnetic rack and waited until the solution became clear.
14. Removed the supernatant and placed in a labeled 1.5mL tube. 
15. Quantified on the Nanodrop
    1. 500uL- 5.5ng/uL, 0.352 260/230, 1.399 260/280
    2. 1mL- 4.979 ng/uL, 0.334 260/230, 1.532 260/280
16. Quantified on the Qubit
    1. 500uL- Out of Range, TOO LOW
    2. 500uL- Out of Range, TOO LOW

CTAB extracted something, but likely not the DNA. Both extractions had very low 260/230 values, indicating extraction buffer or phenol contamination. I was able to remove over 40uL of EB buffer, even though I only put 40uL in meaning I may have accidentally picked up excess wash. 
Both extractions had a 1.4-1.53 260/280 value indicating protein contamination. Indicates I may have accidentally picked up some of the wrong layer from the CI removal step or the CI didn't fully remove the proteins. 
Qubit picks up no DNA (likely because of the charcoal, which was not removed before the extraction) and is probably contaminated creating the disparity between the the Qubit and Nanodrop results.
