## What I did

Sugar Wash (100mL)

***

Sucrose - 58.2g
1M stock Tris-HCL, ph 8- 1mL

20% Triton X-100- 3.75mL
Protease Tablets -x2 (did not add)
DI H2O- 96mL

Combine all ingredients and mix with stir bar. Parafilmed and stored in the fridge.

Centrifugation

***

1\. Pipetted 1mL 4/23/26 JD B cell suspension into a 15mL Falcon tube\. Did the same with 3mL into a separate tube\.
2\. Added Sucrose wash to each tube to equal volume to 10mL\. Vortexed to mix\.
3\. Centrifuged at 1200g for 5 min\. Both tubes pelleted with a slightly darker top layer to the pellet\.
4\. Removed supernatant to best of ability to not disturb bottom layer of the pellet\. Added sugar wash to equal volumes of both tubes to 10mL\. Pipetted to break up pellet and votexed\.
5\. Centrifuged at 1200g for 3 min\. Formed very loose pellets\, no visible different layers but fine dark particles did appear to float in the supernatant\, moreso in the 3mL tube\.
6\. Removed supernatant\, added sucrose wash to equal volumes to 10mL\. Pipetted and vortexed to mix\.
7\. Centrifuged at 1200g for 4 min\. Pellets formed\, but larger particles remained in the supernatant\.
8\. Centrifuged at 800g for 3 min\. Tubes looked exactly the same\.
9\. Centrifuged at 1200g for 3 min\. Top dark layer forms in 3mL tube but not part of the pellet\. 3mL tube also forms a dark top pellet layer\. Many larger particles in both supernatants with a darker color\.
10\. Removed dark top layers from the 3mL tube\. Poured off supernatant from 1mL tube\. Added sugar wash to equal volumes to 10mL\. Pipetted to mix\.
11\. Centrifuged at 1200g for 7 min\. Dark top layer formed on the 3mL tube\. Pellets formed on both with no visible layers\. Lots of floating particles in both\.
12\. Removed dark top layer from 3mL tube and added sugar wash to equal volume to 10mL\. Pipetted to mix \+ break up bottom pellet\. Pipetted to break up pellet in 1mL tube\.
13\. Centrifuged at 900g for 13 minutes\.
14\. Centrifuged at 900g for 7 minutes\. Pellets formed at the bottom of both\. A darker\, finer layer occurred on the side of the 3mL and 1mL tube\. A top dark clump layer appeared in both tubes\, with a fair amount of particles still floating in the sucrose\. 
15\. Removed bottom fine layer of both and added sucrose wash to equal 10mL\. 
16\. Centrifuged at 900g for 15 min\. Same observations as step 14\. 
17\. Removed dark layer from both pellets \(this was the entire 1mL pellet\) and added sucrose wash to equal 10mL\. 
18\. Centrifuged at 1200g for 7 min\. Small pellets formed at the bottom\, big particles at top in solution\.
19\. Placed in freezer\, will continue on thursday\.
