## What I did

1. Defrosted sample 5/21/26 B
2. Pipetted 2mL of sample into 4 2mL tubes. Centrifuged for 2 min at 14000 rpm. 
3. Removed supernatant, added 1.5mL of sucrose wash to each and resuspended via pipetting and vortexing. Centrifuged for 2 min at 14000 rpm.
4. Poured off sucrose wash, resuspended in 1.5mL ultra pure water, and resuspended via pipetting and vortexing. 
5. Pipetted 1.5mL, 1.5mL, 1.25mL, and 1mL from each tube into four new labeled tubes.
6. Centrifuged at max speed (14000 rpm) for 1 minute. Poured off supernatant and resuspended pellets in 150uL buffer A by pipetting.
7. Added 150uL buffer B, then inverted 8 times while tapping to lyse cells.
8. Added 300uL of buffer C, then inverted 8 times to mix.
9. Centrifuged at max speed (14000 rpm) for 10 minutes.
10. Removed supernatant from each tube and place in a spin column in a 2mL collection tube. Centrifuge for 30 seconds at max speed (14000 rpm.) (Discarded tube 1.5A due to accidently putting buffer D in before spinning.)
11. Discarded flow through. Added 650uL Buffer D to each tube then centrifuged for 30 seconds at max speed (14000 rpm.)
12. Discarded flow through. Centrifuged for an additional 30 seconds at max speed (14000 rpm.)
13. Discarded flow through and placed each spin column in a labeled 1.5mL collection tube. Added 50uL EB elution buffer to each column and let sit for 1 minute.
14. Centrifuged for 1 min at max speed, then discarded spin column.
15. Quantified, then stored in freezer.
