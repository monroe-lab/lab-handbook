**CTAB 40um filter extraction:**

1. 4/29/26 DT Monday B was used. 8mL of defrosted suspension was poured into a 40um cell filter atop a 50mL falcon tube. 3mL sucrose wash was pipetted on top of the filter. Filter was tapped to increase flow through.
2. An additional 1mL sucrose was added to the filter and contents were pipetted to disribute and potentially break up large cell/charcoal clumps.
3. Additional 1mL suspension was added and tapped to encourage flow through.
4. Step 3 repeated but pipetted instead. About 10mL of flowthrough was obtained.
5. Tube was centrifuged at 1000rpm for 2 min. Removed bottom pellet.
6. Repeated step 5.
7. Added 100ul 1xPBS to each sample. Pipetted to mix. Then added 12ul RNase A, 3.5 ul Proteinase K, and then 500 ul CTAB. Pipetted to mix. Forgot to turn on tube incubator to 56 C ahead of time (only had to wait \~5 min,) then incubated for 20 min.
8. Added 12ul RNase A to all the samples then incubated at 56 C for another 15 minutes.
9. Centrifuged for 10 min at 4400 g/rcf. Removed supernatant and place into new labeled tubes.
    1. 250ul- 700ul removed
    2. 500ul- 800ul removed
    3. 1mL- 1mL removed
10. Added an equal volume of room temp CI to all tubes, then inverted tubes 20 times. Tubes were then placed on hula mixer for 10 min.
11. Centrifuged for 10 min at 5000 g/rcf. Removed upper aqueous phase and placed into new 1.5mL tubes.
    1. 250ul- 500ul removed
    2. 500ul- 600ul removed
    3. 1mL- 800ul removed
12. Add equal volume 0.4% SeraMag beads to each tube and mix by inversion 20-25 times before incubating at RT for 30 min on the hula mixer.
13. Spun tubes down for 1 min at 5000 g/rcf. Placed on magnetic rack and removed supernatant when solution became clear.
14. Tubes were washed with 80% EtOH by pipetting, vortexing, then spinning and then being placed on the magnetic rack until they became clear (\~2-3 min.) This washing was done twice.
15. DNA was then eluted in 30 ul buffer EB by pipetting, vortexing, then spinning and then placed on the magnetic rack until clear, about 2 min. This buffer was then transfered into labeled tubes for quantification.
16. Quanified on the nanodrop
    1. 1ml- -2.696ng/ul, -0.751 260/230, 0.889 260/280
    2. 500ul- -3.071ng/ul, -14.269 260/230, 0.676 260/280
    3. 250 ul- -3.098ng/ul, 6.356 260/230, 0.949 260/280
17. Quantified on the Qubit
    1. 1ml- Out of range, too low
    2. 500ul- Out of range, too low
    3. 250 ul- Out of range, too low
