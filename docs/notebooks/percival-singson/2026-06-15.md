May want to look into creating a chemical wash to remove charcoal. Dilute HCl, detergent (like 0.1% triton-X or Tween-20,) try using a PBS wash.

Could also investigate methods of removing the charcoal post-lysis. Ideas for this include:

* Alkaline lysis (NaOH/SDS) + high salt buffer (Potassium acetate or Guanidine hydrichloride) (Washing in this forces removal of the DNA from the surface of the charcoal due to salt + pH change)
* This is then centrifuged at max speed for \~5-10 min to separate the charcoal into a pellet from the supernatant of DNA
* Supernatant is the transfered to another tube and 100% cold ethanol is used to precipitate the DNA out and make a pure pellet.

Someone did file for a patent for a method of using activated charcoal for DNA/RNA purification, so hypothetically this method of separation post lysis should work (patent was abandoned so there should be no legal problems for using this.)
