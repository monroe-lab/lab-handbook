**Cell filter + Centrifuge Protocol:**

1. Defrosted 3/3/26 JD A
2. Placed 70um cell filter into two tubes (high + low volume.) Placed 6mL of cell suspension in high volume tube and 3 mL suspension in low volume tube, then added an equal volume of sucrose wash to each.
3. Centrifuged at 1000rpm for 5 min.
4. Poured out flow through. Added equivalent amounts of sucrose wash to each and pipetted to resuspend pellets. Centrifuged for 5 min at 1000 rpm.
5. Repeated step 4, except at 500 rpm for 5 min.
6. Repeated step 5, but used 6mL in the low volume tube.
7. Repeated step 6.
8. Repeated step 6
9. Repeated step 6. Some of one of the tubes (unsure which) one got caught in the centrifuge a bit and spilled. Likely lost some of it's cell contents.
10. Repeated step 6.
11. Repeated step 6.
12. Repeated step 6.
13. Repeated step 6.
14. Repeated step 6.
15. Repeated step 6.
16. Added sucrose wash and pipetted up and down to mix and force through the filter repeatedly on both tubes. 
17. Removed cells from filter and placed in 2mL tubes (they were still rather dark colored.) Suspended in 1mL sucrose wash and pipetted to mix.
18. Centrifuged for 2 min at 800 rpm. 
19. Removed pellet, replaced sucrose wash centrifuged for 2 min at 800rpm. 
20. Repeated step 19, but centrifuged at 1500rpm for 4 minutes.
21. Removed sucrose wash/pellet and then placed onto 40um cell filter and pipetted 2mL sucrose wash on both. Scraped remains from filter and resuspended in 500uL DIH2O. 
22. Counted/looked at on the cell drop:
    1. Low volume: 206 cells per 15uL. Fair amount of charcoal particles still present, including attached to cells.
    2. High volume: 943 cells per 15uL. Fair amount of charcoal particles still present, including a lot attached to the cells.
23. Threw out low volume tube and froze the high volume tube in the DNA sample freezer (the one next to the fridge) in the Percy box.
