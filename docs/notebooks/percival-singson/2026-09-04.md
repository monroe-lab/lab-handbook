## Plan for today

CTAB extraction of grape feeder cells using the G2 DNA/RNA enhancer

## What I did

Total volume of liquid added before first supernatant removal: 1.025mL
Volumes to test: 25uL, 50uL, 99uL

Sample used: 4/30 DT Tuesday B

Sample placed back in the freezer, along with the G2 DNA/RNA Enhancer due to the lab being out of PVP40.

CTAB lysis buffer can be made with PVP36, which has a different molecular weight and can take a while to dissolve. The amount by weight stays the same (for each 10mL made, add 0.1g PVP36 to the solution.) 

Decided to do chemical extraction method instead

Sample used: 5/20 LJ Wednesday A

Made buffers A and C for the chemical extraction method.

Sample sizes: 1.5mL, 1.5mL, 1.25 mL, and 1mL. (1.5mL is the amount used in the original protocol)

1. Pipetted suspension into each labeled tube using a cut pipette tip. Centrifuged for 1 min at maximum speed (14000 rpm.)
2. Poured off supernatant (1.25 still had a little left and 1.5 B had the most removed due to removal by pipetting.) Resuspended each in 150uL buffer A.
3. Added 150uL lysis buffer B to each tube and inverted 8 times to lyse. Required tapping each tube on the bench first to get the clump of cells to move with each inversion.
4. Added 300uL buffer C to each tube and inverted 8 times each to release from the charcoal. Required some tapping due to clumpy, viscous nature.
5. Centrifuged fro 10 minutes at max speed (14000 rpm.)
6. Pipetted supernatant into a spin column in a 2mL collection tube. Discarded black pellet. Centrifuged at max speed (14000 rpm) for 30 seconds.
7. Discarded flow through. Added 650uL buffer D to each column, then centrifuged at max speed (14000 rpm) for 30 seconds. 
8. Discarded flow through. Centrifuged for an additional 30 seconds at maximum speed (14000 rpm.)
9. Removed spin columns and discarded the 2mL collection tubes. Placed spin columns new 1.5mL collection tubes and added 50uL Elution Buffer EB to each tube. Let sit for 1 min, then centrifuged at max speed (14000 rpm) for one minute. Discarded spin columns.
10. Placed samples on ice while waiting to quantify.

This method seems to be able to extract some DNA/thing from the cells but it is very contaminated.
