## Cell Culture Medium Ingredients

\- 20 g/l sucrose

* Used as a food source for the plant cells
* Can interfere with Nanodrop/qubit readings
    * **Can be solved by doing 70% ethanol washes before final elution, commercial spin columns/magnetic beads**

-42 μM picloram

* Used as synthetic auxin in suspension cultures for line maintenance (induces division)
* Can increase nuclease activity and can coprecipitate in alcohol based extractions
    * Therefore it must be fully removed before further extraction steps.
        * **This can be done using PVP, doing multiple washes with 70% ethanol, and CTAB!**

\- 9 μM TDZ

* Used as a synthetic cytokinin
* **Long term exposure can cause frequent somoclonal variations**
* Does not appear to pose issues for DNA extraction

\- 0\.4 M mannitol

* Maintains osmotic environment of the suspension culture, increases efficiency of embryo formation/regeneration
* Does not interfere with DNA extraction, easily washed away with alcohol

\- 222 mg/l CaCl2

* Stabilizes cell walls, mantains osmotic pressure (promotes cell division,) acts as a secondary messanger
* **Can interfere with DNA extraction, can be removed with QIAGEN spin kit, OR an ethanol precipitation with a salt wash (such as sodium acetate) OR EDTA (as a chelator)**

\- 1 g/l casein

* Used as a nitrogen and amino acid supply, used to induce division
* Can precipitate with DNA, can inhibit cell lysis (in high concentrations)
    * **Add a chloroform-isoamyl alcohol (25:24:1) purification step after lysis or a proteinase K step BEFORE lysis**

\- 5 mM HEPES

* Maintains stable pH (5.5-7.5,) particularly preventing drops due to cell growth
* No effect on DNA extraction 

\- 0\.6 mM ascorbic acid

* Mitigates oxidative stress, cofactor for enzymes involved in cell division, hormone biosynthesis, and photosynthesis
* Can drop extraction buffer pH, can interfere with Qubit
    * **Ethanol precipitation and silica column based clean up can be used to remove from final eluate**. Ascorbate can be used to prevent a severe pH drop when conducting an extraction from polyphenol rich samples to prevent polyphenol oxidation.

\- 0\.7 mM citric acid

* Acts as a nutrient chelator, pH buffer, and as an intermediete in cell respiration, energy production, and amino acid/fatty acid synthesis
* Can interfere with DNA extraction but Tris-HCL and EDTA removes it

\- 0\.1 mM reduced glutathione

* Function as an antioxidant against ROS and as redox buffer, used to stimulate embryogenesis
* No effect on DNA extraction

\- 2 g/l activated charcoal\, pH 5\.7

* Absorbs phenolic compounds + toxic compounds, can also inadvertently absorb auxins/cytokinins
* **HIGHLY DNA BINDING, WILL CARRY GENETIC MATERIAL INTO THE PELLET DURING LYSIS**
