# Charcoal Purification DNA Extraction Protocol

*Created by percival-singson on 2026-06-17*

## Required Buffers Recipes

### Buffer A- 2mL stock

* 100uL Tris HCl, pH 8, 1M stock (50mM)
* 40uL EDTA, pH 8, 0.5M stock (10mM)
* 20uL RNase A (100ug/mL)
* 1.84mL DIH2O

### Buffer B- 2mL stock (make in chemical hood)

* 40uL NaOH, 10N stock solution (200mM)
* 200uL SDS, 10% stock solution (1%)
* 1.76mL DIH2O

### Buffer C- 10mL stock (make in chemical hood)

* 4.01g Guanidine Hydrochloride (Gu-HCl) (4.2M)
* 3mL Potassium Acetate, pH 4.8, 3M stock (0.9M)
* DI H2O to 10mL

3M Potassium Acetate stock recipe

* 2.94g Potassium Acetate
* 4mL DI H2O + amount to get to final volume
* Glacial Acetic Acid for pH calibration

### Buffer D- 50mL stock

* 500uL TrisHCl, pH 7.5, 1M stock (10mM)
* 40uL Ethanol (80%)
* 9.5mL DI H2O

## Protocol (Assumes activated charcoal is already present in samples)

1. Centrifuge 1.5mL of the suspension culture at max speed for 1 min.
2. Discard the supernatant and resuspend the mixture in **150uL Buffer A** by pipetting.
3. Add **150uL lysis Buffer B** *in the chemical hood*  to the tube and invert 8 times to lyse the cells.
4. Add **300uL Buffer C** *in the chemical hood* and invert 8 times to release the DNA from the charcoal.
5. Centrifuge at max speed for 10 minutes to form a black pellet of the charcoal
6. Remove the supernatant and place it in a new spin column in a 2mL collection tube. Centrifuge for 30 seconds at max speed to bind the DNA to the column. Discard the flow throug.
7. Add **650uL Buffer D** to the spin column and centrifuge at max speed for 30 sec. Discard the flow through. 
8. Centrifuge at max speed for 30 more seconds and discard the remaining flow through.
9. Place the spin column in a 1.5mL collection tube and add **50uL EB buffer**. Wait 1 minute, then centrifuge for 1 minute at maximum speed. Discard spin column. 
10. Store DNA in the freezer for future use if not quantifying immediately.
