# Charcoal CTAB

## CTAB 50mL stock recipe

* 5mL Tris-HCl, 1M stock (100mM)
* 2mL EDTA, 0.5M stock (20mM)
* 14uL NaCl, 5M stock
* 0.8g CTAB 2%
* DI H2O up to 50 mL

For each 10mL used, add 0.1g PVP-40 1%, *RIGHT BEFORE USE*

## Protocol

1. Defrost cell culture samples.
2. Remove 2mL of culture via pipetting with a cut tip and place into a new 2mL tube.
3. Centrifuge cultures for 2 minutes at 14000 rpm.
4. Remove medium supernatant (Very important to remove all of it.)
5. Resuspend cells in 1.5mL of sucrose solution by pipetting and vortexing.
6. Centrifuge cultures for 90 seconds at 14000rpm.
7. Remove supernatant (Very important to remove all of it.)
8. Resuspend cells in 1.5mL ultrapure DI H2O by pipetting and vortexing. 
9. Add 500uL G2 DNA/RNA Liquidized Enhancer to each tube.
10. Add 10uL 10% SDS to each tube.
11. Add 500ul CTAB lysis buffer, 12ul RNase A, and 3.5 ul Proteinase K to each sample and pipette to mix. Incubate at 55C for 20 minutes.
12. Centrifuge at 4400g for 10 min, then transfer the supernatant to a new tube, keeping track of the volume that is transferred.
13. Add an equal volume of room temp CI to each tube in the fume hood, and mix by inverting 20 times. Incubate for 10 min on the Hula Mixer.
14. Centrifuge samples at 5000g for 10 min at room temperature.
15. In the fume hood, transfer the upper aqueous phase to a new 1.5 mL tube.
16. Add an equal volume of 0.4% SeraMag beads to each tube, Mix by inversion 20-25 times and incubate at room temperature for 30 min on the Hula mixer.
17. Spin tubes down for 1 min at 5000g and place on a magenetic rack. Wait for solution to become clear and discard the supernatant. Place EB buffer in the incubator to warm
18. Pipette \~1-2mL 80% EtOH into the tubes, pipette to mix, vortex, then spin down. Place back on metal rack, and wait until the solution is clear then discard the supernatant.
19. Repeat step 10.
20. Add 30-50ul prewarmed EB to each tube, pipette to mix, vortex, then spin down and place back on metal rack. Wait until the solution is clear and remove the eluted DNA in the EB buffer and place in a labeled 1.5mL collection tube.
21. Quantify, or place in the freezer for later use.
